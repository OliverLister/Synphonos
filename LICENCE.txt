## Privacy notice

The rest of this document explains what Synphonos does with information.
We are not required to publish a privacy policy: the Privacy Act 1988
(Cth) applies to organisations above a turnover threshold that Synphonos
is nowhere near. It is written anyway, because you should be able to know
what a program does with your information before you run it.


## 11. The short answer

Synphonos does not collect anything, does not send anything anywhere, and
does not connect to the internet. There is no account, no sign-in, no
registration, no licence check, no update check, no analytics, no
telemetry, no crash reporting service and no advertising. Nothing you load
into it, and nothing about your show, leaves your machine.

The only outbound thing Synphonos ever does is ask Windows to open a web
page in your browser, and only when you click one of the links in the
program: the loopMIDI download page, the Symphonia source repository, or
the donation page. That is your browser making that connection, on your
instruction, and the site you land on has its own privacy policy.


## 12. What Synphonos writes on your own machine

These files stay on your machine. We never see them.

- settings.toml, in your user profile's application data folder, under
  tcgen. It holds your audio device and MIDI port names, your key
  assignments, the colours you chose, the show last open and the playhead
  position.
- Show files, with the extension .synshow, wherever you save them. They
  hold your set list, the paths of your audio files, the start offsets and
  the marks.
- crash.log, beside settings.toml, written only if the program falls over.
  It holds the reason and where in the program it happened.

Be aware that these files contain file paths, and that a Windows file path
usually contains your account name, and may contain a client's or a
production's name. That matters only if you choose to send one on.


## 13. If you send us something

Nothing reaches us unless you send it. If you do choose to send us a

If you donate, the donation is handled by the donation service, who are

We do not sell anything about you, do not share it for advertising, and do
not use it to make any automated decision.
